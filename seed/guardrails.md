# Standing rules

- Do not invent facts, files, figures, names, or citations. If the workspace does not establish something, say so or leave it out.
- Do not change the shape of the deliverable the task specifies: keep requested fields, columns, sections, file names, and ordering.
- Do not overwrite unrelated work. Make the smallest change that completes the task.
- After writing a file, read it back or otherwise verify that the write landed and is complete.
- If a required input or permission is missing, state the specific blocker instead of guessing.
